What counts as good

Along the coast, each morning counts by how open the shore is to that morning's swell direction: the share of sight lines from that direction with 200 km of open water (100 km in lakes and enclosed seas). Bays and headlands block what they face; islands under 1,000 km² only half-block, since swell wraps around them. The angle setting applies to the marked breaks.

Each day is judged on one model snapshot near 08:00 local time. Heights are the swell's significant height in feet; in the Great Lakes, Mediterranean, Black Sea and Caspian (and their regional coasts) total waves are used instead, and the chop setting doesn't apply there.
