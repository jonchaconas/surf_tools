    What counts as good

    Breaks: tide
    lowmidhigh

    Tide at the breaks is each morning's level within that break's own range (EOT20 tide model): 0% is a low tide, 100% a high one. Breaks with a known swell window (Pipeline, Uluwatu, Jeffreys Bay…) use it instead of the angle setting.

    At the marked breaks, either swell train can make a morning good, and the wind setting uses ERA5 morning wind just offshore; wind from the land (within 45° of straight offshore) always counts as clean. Out at sea and in the coloured coastal band, the chop setting judges the wave model's wind sea instead.

    Along the coast, each morning counts by how open the shore is to that morning's swell direction: the share of sight lines from that direction with 200 km of open water (100 km in lakes and enclosed seas). Bays and headlands block what they face; islands under 1,000 km² only half-block, since swell wraps around them.

    Each day is judged on one model snapshot near 08:00 local time. Heights are the swell's significant height in feet; in the Great Lakes, Mediterranean, Black Sea and Caspian (and their regional coasts) total waves are used instead, and neither wind nor chop applies there.
